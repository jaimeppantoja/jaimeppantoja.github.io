About Me

I am a Master’s candidate in Computer Science with a focus on Artificial Intelligence, specializing in data analytics and machine learning. As an independent research graduate student at the University of Wolverhampton, I have developed analytical projects in the real estate and financial industries, using advanced technologies to create insightful reports and interactive dashboards.

Vancouver Housing Market Analytics

The Vancouver Housing Market Analytics project is an advanced analytical report designed to provide comprehensive insights into the real estate market in Vancouver. This project uses web scraping to gather periodic housing data, including prices, trends, and statistical information. By analyzing this data, the project aims to offer a clear and detailed overview of the current state of the Vancouver housing market.

Financial Insights Dashboard

The financial data dashboard delivers in-depth insights into stock market trends and key financial metrics, providing a holistic view of price movements, correlations, and variations across selected stocks.

Designed for intuitive analysis, the dashboard allows users to explore dynamic trends and identify patterns with ease. In addition, it features an integrated news feed, offering real-time, contextual updates on the companies being analyzed, enriching the decision-making process with timely, relevant information.
